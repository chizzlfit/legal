# Chizzl Support

Need help with Chizzl? Get in touch and we'll get back to you as soon as we can.

**Email: chizzlfit@gmail.com**

When you write in, it helps to include:
- Your account email
- Your device and iOS version
- A short description of what happened (screenshots welcome)

## Common questions

**Managing or cancelling your subscription** — open the **Settings** app on your iPhone, tap your name, then **Subscriptions**, and select Chizzl.

**Restoring a purchase** — open the paywall in the App and tap **Restore purchases**.

**Deleting your account** — in the App, go to **Profile → Delete Account**. This permanently removes your account and data.
